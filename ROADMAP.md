# Guess Arcade Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not guaranteed dates.

## Shipped

- Solo Classic, Sprint, Survival, Endless, and Daily modes
- Two-player Duel and local 3–6 player tournaments
- Challenge codes/links, achievements, streaks, profiles, and local history
- Installable offline PWA with backup/restore

## Improving

- mobile and accessibility polish
- cross-browser PWA verification
- real-device party-mode QA

## Exploring

- additional deterministic challenges
- more local tournament formats
- new arcade modifiers that stay backend-free

## Product boundary

New infrastructure or third-party services should earn their complexity. Preserve the current product model unless a concrete user need justifies changing it.
